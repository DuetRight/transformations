Our approach

Start with the room.

Stan visits the house and runs the build. Angelo brings a mechanical engineer’s eye to the scope. Austin works through the estimate and selections with you. The person who spots a problem on site can bring it straight back to the drawing.

Measure what’s there

The first model starts with the house. We measure openings, fixtures and clearances. Photos, scans and an existing-condition plan give us a common starting point. We keep track of the measurements we can verify and what we still need to see behind the wall.

Try the choices together

Can you reach the shower valve from the entry? Will a drawer clear the door? We can draw the alternatives, turn the Blender model on screen, and make stills or a short film to compare the views that matter to you.

Work out how to build it

We use the model to break the work into demolition, framing, plumbing, waterproofing and finishes. The agreed layout feeds the drawings, material quantities, estimate and schedule. If you change a detail after installation, we explain the rework and get your written approval before proceeding.
