Our approach

From your first sketch to the last tile.

A calm, modern room starts with what you need to do there. You work with the same small team on the layout, the estimate and the build. When a detail changes, the people drawing it can work through the cost and the installation with you.

Start with your day

Show us where the door gets in the way or what ends up on the counter. We measure the room, take photographs and use a 3D scan where it helps. Those measurements give us a plan to draw on together.

Walk through the choices

In Blender, we can move a wall, change a finish or test the reach to a shower valve. You can turn the model, compare renders from the same spot, or follow a video walkthrough before settling on a layout.

Work out the build

We turn the agreed design into drawings, material quantities and an itemized estimate. A cabinet section shows where a drawer clears a drain. A framing plan shows where a grab bar will hold. We can revise those details with you before anything is installed.
